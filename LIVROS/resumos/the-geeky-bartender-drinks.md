# The Geeky Bartender Drinks: Real-Life Recipes for Fantasy Cocktails

**Author:** Cassandra Reeder ("The Geeky Chef")
**Publisher:** Race Point Publishing / Quarto Publishing Group, Minneapolis, 2020, ISBN 9780760367933

Cassandra Reeder runs the long-running pop-culture recipe blog *The Geeky Chef* (est. 2008), which recreates fictional foods and drinks from books, games, movies, and TV. This book is her dedicated cocktail volume: every recipe is a real-world drink engineered to match the color, flavor concept, in-world description, or narrative role of a beverage from a specific fandom — Skyrim mead, Klingon bloodwine, the Pan Galactic Gargle Blaster, Butterbeer, Nuka-Cola, and dozens more. Each entry opens with a short essay on the source material and the design logic behind the recipe, followed by an ingredient list, numbered method, suggested serving vessel/garnish, and (for most) a **Virginize** variant that removes the alcohol.

## Philosophy and Technique Synthesis

**Design method: translate fiction into flavor/color/effect, not literal ingredients.** Reeder's consistent approach is to take an in-world description (a color, a stated effect, a vague flavor note, a plot function) and reverse-engineer a real cocktail that evokes it — e.g., Star Trek's *Samarian Sunset* (a drink that "explodes with color" when vibrated) is recreated using the sake-bomb technique of dropping a shot glass of colored liquid into a base drink balanced on chopsticks; *A Clockwork Orange*'s drugged "Moloko Plus" becomes an orange-cream cocktail; Fallout's *Nuka-Cola Quantum* uses Hawaiian Punch and Sunny D as flavor bases because they're already "entirely artificial" like the in-game soda.

**Five practical rules from the introduction:**
1. Always use a cocktail shaker — "the mystical effects... can't be understated."
2. Don't be afraid of bitters — treated as the seasoning layer that finishes a drink.
3. Always keep simple syrups on hand as the primary sweetening/flavoring method.
4. Adjust ratios freely — "crafting cocktails is not an objective science."
5. Buy in bulk what you'll use often; buy mini bottles to test unfamiliar liqueurs (which double as reusable "potion bottles").

**The Virginize convention.** Nearly every alcoholic recipe carries a parallel nonalcoholic substitution (e.g., replace bourbon with extra white tea, rum with water plus flavoring, blue curaçao with blue Hawaiian Punch), making the book usable as a dual cocktail/mocktail reference.

**Presentation as core content, not garnish afterthought.** The dedicated "Special Effects" chapter treats glass-rimming, ice design, citrus-peel spirals, edible shimmer dust, dry-ice mist, and flaming garnishes as first-class techniques, each recipe then cross-referencing which effect(s) suit it (e.g., the Pan Galactic Gargle Blaster is built with a literal sugar-cube "Algolian Suntiger tooth," dry-ice mist, and a citrus spiral, treating the source text's joke ingredients as a build sheet).

**Explicit safety framing around dry ice and fire.** The book is unusually careful for a novelty title: dry ice must never be touched or ingested even after it's dropped in a drink; flaming cocktails must never be drunk while still lit, never blown out (spreads the fire), and require heatproof glass, cleared flammables, and a fire extinguisher on hand for The Flaming Float.

## Component Library (Syrups, referenced throughout)

- **Classic Simple Syrup** — 1 cup sugar : 1 cup water, boiled 3–5 minutes until dissolved
- **Grenadine** — 1 cup pomegranate juice, ¾ cup sugar, 1 tsp lemon juice, 2–3 drops orange flower water, simmered ~20 minutes to thicken
- **Berry Syrup** — 1 cup fresh/frozen berries, 1 cup water, ½ cup sugar, 1 tsp lemon zest, simmered 15 minutes, strained
- **Lavender Syrup** — 3 tbsp dried lavender buds, 1 cup water, 1½ cups sugar, simmered then steeped 45 minutes
- **Ginger Syrup** — ¾ cup sliced fresh ginger, 1 cup water, 1½ cups sugar, simmered 15 minutes
- **Cinnamon Syrup** — 5 cinnamon sticks, 1 cup water, 1 cup sugar, simmered and steeped 45 minutes
- **Honey Syrup** — ¾ cup water, ¾ cup honey, warmed (not boiled) until dissolved
- **Brown Sugar Syrup** — 1 cup water, 1½ cups dark brown sugar, 1 tsp vanilla extract
- **Spiced Maple Syrup** — ½ cup maple syrup, ½ cup water, 2 whole allspice, 2 cinnamon sticks, 2 whole cloves, 2 star anise, steeped 45 minutes

## Recipe Sample — Organized by Chapter

### Otherworldly Intoxicants

**White-Gold Tower** (The Elder Scrolls/Skyrim; serves 1) — two kinds of mead, whiskey (optional), bitters, and Lavender Syrup shaken with ice and poured into a highball glass, topped with a hand-poured layered float of heavy cream, garnished with an edible flower (nasturtium, orchid, or viola, to mimic in-game "Dragon's Tongue").

**Velvet LeChance** (The Elder Scrolls/Skyrim; serves 1) — 5 blackberries muddled with 1–2 tsp Honey Syrup, then 2 oz crème de mûre (blackberry liqueur) and 4 oz bottled spiced/mulled wine stirred in, finished with a tiny pinch of cayenne pepper (standing in for the drink's "nightshade" note).

**The Yellow Fairy** (Fable II; serves 1) — 1½ oz whipped-cream or vanilla vodka, 1 oz overproof white rum, ½ oz amaretto, 2 oz pineapple juice, 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, 1–2 tsp Brown Sugar Syrup, shaken with ice and strained; garnished with a dollop of Marshmallow Fluff and a lemon spiral.

**Noble Pursuit** (The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild; serves 2–3) — 3 cups fresh watermelon blended and strained, combined with 2 oz cold water, 2 oz light rum, 2 oz gin, 4 tsp Lavender Syrup, juice of 1 lime, and ice, stirred and poured; garnished with lavender-bud ice. *Virginize: omit rum/gin, add extra chopped watermelon and water.*

**Sulfuron Slammer** (World of Warcraft; serves 1) — 2 oz gold tequila, 1 oz Ancho Reyes, ½ oz amaretto, 2 oz pineapple juice, 2–3 dashes Angostura, shaken with ice; garnished with jalapeño slices and set aflame using The Flaming Float technique.

**Grog** (Monkey Island; serves 2) — 3 oz spiced rum, 1 oz coconut rum, 4 oz pineapple juice, 3 oz orange juice, 2–3 tsp Cinnamon Syrup, juice of 1–2 limes, a pinch of sea salt, stirred with ice; optionally 1 oz blue curaçao and green food coloring for the series' signature acid-green color.

### Magical Elixirs

**Amor di Infierno** (Grimm; serves 1) — 1 oz Chartreuse, 1 oz pear juice, 1 tsp Honey Syrup, a dash of Angostura bitters shaken with ice and strained into a champagne flute, topped with 3–4 oz brut champagne.

**Chateau Romani** (The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask; serves 2) — 1 cup whole milk, 2 oz bourbon, 2 oz Irish cream liqueur, 1 oz coffee liqueur, 2 small scoops vanilla ice cream, 1 tsp vanilla extract, a pinch each of cinnamon and nutmeg, blended smooth.

**Hero Drink** (Final Fantasy; serves 1–2) — 2 oz light rum, 1 oz banana schnapps, 1–2 oz orange juice, 2 oz pineapple juice, shaken hard ~20 seconds; garnished with a yellow Sparkly Rim to match the series' "Chocobo yellow" potion color.

**Miruvor** (The Lord of the Rings; serves 1) — 3 oz lightly brewed hot white tea, 1½ oz elderflower liqueur, 1½ oz gin (Plymouth or London Dry), 2–3 tsp Honey Syrup, 1 tsp orange blossom water, stirred directly in the serving vessel.

**Red Potion** (generic video-game; serves 1) — 1½ oz silver tequila, 1 oz Campari, ½ oz sweet vermouth, 1 oz cran-raspberry juice shaken with ice, topped with 2 oz chilled soda water; garnished with strawberries cut into heart shapes.

**Blue Potion** (generic video-game; serves 1) — 1 oz white rum, 1 oz coconut rum, ¾ oz blue curaçao, 3 oz sparkling coconut water, 1 oz fresh lime juice, 1 oz simple syrup stirred with ice, topped with chilled lemon-lime soda.

**Green Potion** (generic video-game; serves 1) — 1 tbsp matcha powder dissolved in warm water, shaken with 1 oz vodka, 1 oz Midori, and ice; poured over a scoop of lime sherbet and topped with lemon-lime soda.

**Giggle Water** (Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them; serves 6) — 3 oz bourbon, 1 oz butterscotch schnapps, 2 oz nonalcoholic spiced apple cider stirred with ice, divided among shot glasses, each topped with champagne — "drink in one gulp, laugh uncontrollably."

### Sci-Fi Spirits

**Ryncol Cocktail** (Mass Effect; serves 1) — 1½ oz overproof white rum, 1½ oz Midori, ½ oz absinthe, ½ oz simple syrup, 1–1½ oz fresh lime juice, a drop of mint extract shaken with ice, strained and topped with lemon-lime soda; rimmed with sky-blue sugar.

**Shimmerwine** (Firefly; serves 2) — 2 oz St-Germain elderflower liqueur and 1 oz vodka or gin split between two flutes, topped with 8 oz chilled champagne.

**Mudder's Milk** (Firefly; serves 1–2) — a blended "meal in a glass": 2 oz bourbon, 2 oz Irish cream liqueur, 1 cup whole milk, ½ cup plain Greek yogurt, 1 frozen banana, 2 tbsp peanut butter, ¼ cup oatmeal, flax and chia seeds, Spiced Maple Syrup, cinnamon, and crushed ice, blended smooth.

**Bloodwine** (Star Trek/Klingon; serves 1) — 1½ oz dry sherry, 1 oz overproof spiced rum, ½ oz cherry liqueur, 2–3 tsp Grenadine, ½–1 oz fresh lemon juice, 2–3 dashes orange bitters, shaken and strained.

**Samarian Sunset** (Star Trek; serves 1) — 2 oz coconut water shaken with 1 oz white rum and strained into a highball; separately, ¾ oz shimmer-effect white rum, ½ oz pineapple juice, and ½ oz grenadine are loaded into a shot glass balanced on chopsticks across the glass rim, then slammed sake-bomb style so the shot drops in and "explodes" into orange.

**Romulan Ale** (Star Trek; serves 1–2) — 2 oz overproof white rum, 1 oz blue curaçao, ½–1 oz fresh lemon juice shaken with ice, topped with 3 oz chilled ginger beer.

**Ardees aka Jawa Juice** (Star Wars; serves 1) — 2 oz rye whiskey, 3–4 tsp Spiced Maple Syrup, ½ oz fresh lemon juice, 2–3 dashes Peychaud's bitters shaken with ice, topped with chilled bacon-infused ale; rimmed with bacon salt.

**Tatooine Sunset** (Star Wars; serves 1) — silver tequila and blue curaçao stirred separately; pomegranate liqueur and grenadine built over cracked ice with lemon and orange juice; the tequila/curaçao mix poured gently over a spoon on top for a sunset-layer effect; garnished with two cherries "for both of Tatooine's suns."

**Pan Galactic Gargle Blaster** (The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy; serves 1) — a sugar cube muddled with lemon/grapefruit bitters and tonic water in the glass, then built with fresh lemon juice, 1 oz moonshine, 2 oz London dry gin, a drop of crème de menthe, and remaining tonic, finished with a small piece of dry ice via The Mist Effect (never consumed directly).

**Spice Beer** (Dune; serves 1–2) — 1½ oz cinnamon liqueur, 1½ oz bourbon, 1 tsp lemon juice, Peychaud's bitters, and Cinnamon Syrup stirred, topped with chilled IPA.

### Culty Cocktails

**Sonic Screwdriver** (Doctor Who; serves 1) — 2 oz vodka, 1 oz blue curaçao, 2 tsp lemon juice, simple syrup to taste, stirred with ice and topped with orange-flavored sparkling water; blue Sparkly Rim.

**Emergency Protocol 417** (Doctor Who; serves 1) — a vermouth-heavy martini: 1½ oz gin, 1½ oz dry vermouth stirred with ice, strained, garnished with a Spanish olive.

**White Russian** (The Big Lebowski; serves 1) — 2 oz vodka, 1½ oz coffee liqueur, 1½ oz half-and-half over cracked ice, stirred.

**Black Yukon Sucker Punch** (Twin Peaks; serves 1–2) — bourbon, Yukon Jack (or other honey liqueur), cherry liqueur, chilled bold coffee, and Spiced Maple Syrup shaken and strained into a highball; topped with a whipped "blue foam" made from egg whites (or, as a substitute, whipped heavy cream), blue curaçao, lemon juice, cream of tartar, and more syrup, spooned on top and shaped into two peaks.

**Three-Toed Sloth** (*Cocktail*, the Tom Cruise film; serves 1) — 1 oz each sloe gin, cognac, crème de cacao, and Kahlúa with chocolate bitters, shaken and poured, topped with a hand-layered heavy cream float and 3 coffee beans ("toes").

**Vesper** (James Bond/*Casino Royale*; serves 1) — 3 oz Gordon's gin, 1 oz vodka, ½ oz Lillet Blanc or Cocchi Americano (a modern substitute for the discontinued Kina Lillet), shaken ~20 seconds and strained; garnished with a lemon spiral.

**Singapore Sling** (Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas; serves 1) — 1½ oz gin, 1 oz cherry brandy, ½ oz Bénédictine, ½ oz Triple Sec, 3 oz pineapple juice, 2 tsp Grenadine, a squeeze of lime, a dash of orange bitters, shaken and strained over cracked ice.

**Black Frost Beer** (Buffy the Vampire Slayer; serves 1) — 1 oz absinthe, Ginger Syrup, grapefruit bitters, and a squeeze of lime stirred in a beer glass, topped with chilled light lager for foam.

### Literary Libations

**Butterbeer** (Harry Potter; serves 3–4) — stout beer simmered with cinnamon sticks, cloves, and allspice, then stirred with imitation and melted butter, vanilla extract, brown sugar, evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk, and 3–4 oz spiced rum; served warm in mugs with whipped cream and cinnamon-stick garnish.

**Firewhisky** (Harry Potter; serves 1) — 1 oz rye whiskey, ½ oz Triple Sec, 1½ oz cinnamon whiskey, Cinnamon Syrup, and orange bitters stirred over ice; garnished with The Citrus Flame (flamed orange peel).

**Oosquai** (The Wheel of Time; serves 2) — 4 oz bourbon, 1 oz sweet vermouth, 5–6 dashes Angostura bitters stirred with ice and strained; garnished with a flamed orange spiral.

**Couzi** (Demon Cycle; serves 4) — 3 oz strong rye whiskey, 1 oz cinnamon liqueur, Cinnamon Syrup, ground cinnamon or Peychaud's bitters, and lemon juice stirred with ice, divided among four shot glasses.

**Violet Wine** (The Stormlight Archive; serves 1) — 1 oz crème de violette, ½ oz raspberry liqueur, ½ oz lemon juice shaken with ice and strained, topped with 4 oz dry rosé wine.

**Mulled Wine** (A Song of Ice and Fire/Game of Thrones; serves 8–10) — 2 bottles red wine and optional brandy simmered gently (not boiled) with orange and lemon zest/juice, fresh ginger, cinnamon sticks, cloves, peppercorns, whole nutmeg, allspice, and honey for about an hour, then strained.

**Scumble and Fluff** (Discworld; serves 1–2) — 1 oz applejack brandy, apple bitters, Spiced Maple Syrup, and optional Apple Pie Moonshine stirred over ice ("Scumble"); add bitter ale to turn it into "Fluff."

**Strawberry Cordial** (Redwall; serves 2–4) — sugar, water, and fresh strawberries simmered and blended, strained into a pitcher, then combined with 4 oz white rum and a full 750 ml bottle of chilled prosecco.

### Dystopian Potions

**Victory Gin** (1984; serves 1) — 1 oz dry gin, a dash each of dry vermouth, mirin, and Angostura bitters, plus a squeeze of lemon, shaken with ice.

**Moloko Plus** (A Clockwork Orange; serves 1–2) — fresh orange juice, half-and-half, vanilla vodka, white rum, and Triple Sec blended with crushed ice into a milkshake-like drink.

**Atomic Cocktail** (Fallout: New Vegas; serves 1) — chilled pineapple soda and Red Bull stirred with Grenadine and fresh lime juice, then a shot of vodka bombed into the glass.

**Nuka-Cola Dark** (Fallout 4: Nuka-World; serves 1–2) — black vodka, spiced rum, raspberry liqueur, and chocolate bitters stirred with ice, strained and topped with cola.

**Dirty Wastelander** (Fallout 4; serves 1–2) — blackberries and blueberries muddled with simple syrup and lemon juice, built with whiskey over ice and topped with cola.

**SoulStorm Brew** (Oddworld; serves 1–2) — lime Jell-O mix dissolved in hot water, combined with light rum, melon liqueur, a pinch of sea salt, and ice, topped with lemon-lime soda.

### Comedic Concoctions

**Peppermint Patty** (Archer; serves 3–4) — hot cocoa stirred with vanilla extract, half-and-half, dark crème de cacao, crème de menthe, and peppermint schnapps, served warm.

**6 Gummy Bears and Some Scotch** (Archer; serves 2) — gummy bears soaked in Scotch whisky for at least 15 hours until saturated, then split between two glasses each with 2 oz fresh Scotch.

**Swanky-Panky** (Bob's Burgers; serves 1) — a Hanky-Panky riff: 2 oz sweet vermouth, 1 oz dry gin, 2 dashes Peychaud's bitters stirred with ice and strained; orange peel oils expressed over the top.

**Moss Wine** (Brooklyn Nine-Nine; serves 1) — basil muddled with lemon juice, then shaken with dry white wine, vermouth, and Ginger Syrup over ice.

**Flanders' Planter's Punch** (The Simpsons; serves 2–3) — 4½ oz spiced rum, 1½ oz bourbon, crème de cassis, pineapple and orange juice, water, lime juice, Grenadine, and Angostura bitters, stirred with ice and served over more ice in a plastic cup.

**Fight Milk** (It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia; serves 1–2) — vanilla vodka, whole milk, egg protein powder, malted milk powder, and Brown Sugar Syrup blended with crushed ice.

**Beer Milkshake** (Red Dwarf; serves 2–4) — chocolate stout, crème de cacao, chocolate ice cream, and chocolate sauce blended briefly, poured into chocolate-sauce-lined glasses.

**Alaskan Polar Bear Heater** (The Nutty Professor, 1963; serves 1–2) — vodka, rum, orange bitters, apple cider vinegar, vermouth, gin, brandy, and Scotch all shaken together and poured into a tall glass — "drink, and promptly pass out."

### Nonalcoholic Beverages

**Blue Milk** (Star Wars; serves 1) — goat's milk, avocado, blueberry syrup, vanilla ice cream, and blue food coloring blended smooth.

**Lon Lon Milk** (The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time; serves 2) — milk, chopped green apple (or applesauce), vanilla frozen yogurt, and caramel sauce blended together.

**Slurm** (Futurama; serves 1) — mint jelly and frozen limeade blended, then combined with soda water and optional green food coloring.

**Ent Draught** (The Lord of the Rings; serves 1) — mint leaves muddled in maple syrup, combined with lemon juice, a drop of almond extract, and chilled lightly brewed green tea.

**Nuka-Cola Quantum** (Fallout; serves 1–2) — a Hawaiian-Punch/Sunny-D base with added pomegranate syrup, engineered to mimic a glowing, artificially fruity radioactive soda.
