## Content

Cover page (p.1) indexes three ingredient wheels with page refs and a reading key (hexagon = a pairing around the hub; dashed hexagon = "wild card"; superscript number = see notes below the wheel).

### 1. Peach (p.2) — "the sweet component, vanilla to smoky spirits"
10 pairings around the hub: Vanilla, Cinnamon, Honey, Lemon, Ginger, Raspberry, Prosciutto/Bacon, Mint, Lapsang Souchong, Smoky Spirits.
Technique notes: fat-wash the cocktail with vanilla-extract yogurt; grilled peaches + cinnamon + a dark spirit; use honey as the sweetener instead of sugar; a Buck/Mule with ginger beer + mint, or a fruity Penicillin twist with smoky Scotch; foamy builds pairing peach (sweet) + gin + neutral acid + herbal modifier + raspberry foam, or a shaken sour in Clover Club style; fat-wash the spirit then pair with prosciutto/bacon; Lapsang Souchong keeps the smoky profile in zero-proof drinks when smoky spirits are swapped out.

### 2. Tomato (p.3) — "mostly for Bloody Mary / Red Snapper twists"
13 pairings + one wild card: Plums, Balsamic Vinegar, Worcestershire Sauce, Mustard Seeds, Dill/Parsley, Cilantro, Mushrooms, Oregano, Chili/Jalapeño, Red Onion, Parmesan, Oysters, Olives/Olive Brine, and wild card Passion Fruit.
Technique notes: add a bit of PX sherry to a Bloody Mary; balsamic vinegar note extends to "basically most vinegar-based sauces"; mushrooms suggest a "mushroom-tomato ketchup" flavor profile; oysters work mostly as a side snack/pairing. Overall guidance ("nota bene"): use these flavors to build a custom seasonal Bloody Mary premix.

### 3. Basil (p.4) — "pairs beautifully with fats — and umami"
12 pairings: Peanut Butter, Avocado, Shiitake Mushrooms, Yellow Bell Pepper, Artichoke, Grapefruit, Watermelon, Cucumber, Green Apple/Malic Acid, Passion Fruit, Lemongrass, Pear.
Technique notes: fat-wash gin or vodka with peanut butter and build the basil cocktail around it; avocado's fat gives basil great texture; yellow bell pepper ≈ sweet pepper or Bulgarian paprika; add a splash of Cynar to a Basil Smash or a "Basil Suzette"; grapefruit works mostly as an oleo-saccharum (same logic as gentian root in Suze); cucumber follows the "same-color things work together" rule of thumb (like watermelon); green apple/malic acid brings a tart, unripe edge — sour apples work best; passion fruit pairs perfectly with umami, so basil acts as seasoning rather than the lead flavor; pear + Gorgonzola + basil flagged as a standout combo.
Teaser for a follow-up post: "neon-green herb syrups that last twice as long" + 5 summer recipes including a Basil Suzette.
