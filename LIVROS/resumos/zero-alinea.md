# ZERO: A New Approach to Non-Alcoholic Drinks

**Authors:** Grant Achatz, Allen Hemberger, Nick Kokonas (with Micah Melton) — The Alinea Group
**Published:** First edition, Chicago, 2020. ISBN-13: 978-1-7330088-1-5
**Publisher:** The Alinea Group, LLC. Design & photography by Sarah & Allen Hemberger (Small Batch Creative).

This is the non-alcoholic beverage book from the team behind Alinea, Next, The Aviary, The Office, and Roister — Chicago's flagship fine-dining/cocktail restaurant group. It is not a "mocktail" book in the syrupy sense; it applies the group's culinary R&D methodology (the same one used for Alinea's tasting menus and The Aviary's cocktail program) to drinks that contain zero alcohol. Recipes originated in restaurant service but were rewritten/tested for home kitchens.

## 1. Structural Overview

The book is ~250 pages, organized into 5 chapters plus front matter (Introductions, "A Culinary Approach to Beverage Design," "Before We Begin," "Basic Syrups"):

| Chapter | Page range | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Mise en Place | 6–24 | Philosophy essay + equipment + basic syrups |
| Ch.1 — Back Bar | 25–44 | 14 non-alcoholic "spirit" building blocks (in the style of Gin, Whiskey, Tequila, Rum, Mezcal, vermouths, amari, bitters, liqueurs) |
| Ch.2 — Classic Cocktails | 45–64 | Non-alcoholic riffs on Mimosa, Paloma, Daiquiri, Jungle Bird, Margarita, Absinthe drip, French 75, Whiskey Sour, Black Manhattan, Amaro & Coke, Bijou, Negroni |
| Ch.3 — Modern Cocktails | 65–165 | ~30 original, restaurant-service drinks (Alinea, Next, The Aviary, The Office, Roister, St. Clair Supper Club) |
| Ch.4 — Wines | 167–206 | ~13 wine-analog drinks (Champagne, shrub, port, dessert-wine styles) |
| Ch.5 — Etcetera | 207–236 | Beer, milkshake, tea, horchata, and other non-cocktail formats |
| Index | 237+ | |

Each recipe is built from **sub-components** ("stocks," "syrups," "batches") that can be made ahead and refrigerated, mirroring how the restaurants mise-en-place a service. A finished cocktail is usually: `[stock/tea/juice extraction] → [batch/base — sweetened & acid-balanced] → [to portion/assemble & serve]`. Every backbar recipe yields ~1 qt (1 L); most cocktail batches yield ~1 qt and are portioned to order.

## 2. Technique & Philosophy Synthesis

The 16-page opening essay ("A Culinary Approach to Beverage Design") is the intellectual core of the book. Key ideas:

- **Alcohol removal breaks balance, not just flavor.** A daiquiri is rum+lime+sugar in perfect balance; removing rum leaves an acidic, cloying drink. Water fills the *volume* but none of the rum's *function* (flavor carrier, viscosity, burn, sweetness counterweight). The design task is replacing alcohol's *roles*, not its taste.
- **Design axes considered for every drink:** Occasion (time of day, food pairing, portion count), Flavor & Aroma (borrow from the culinary world, not just the bar canon — mole, salad, oatmeal, fried chicken), Visual Aesthetics (glassware sets expectations independent of contents), Seasoning & Balance (adjust acid/sugar/salt/bitterness separately, as in cooking — most recipes intentionally under-season the base and season to taste at the end), Texture (mouthfeel — grippy tannins vs. thin/cleansing), Form Factor (should a flavor be presented as a "cocktail," a "wine," or a "beer"?), Serving Size (assertive flavors get smaller pours), Temperature (chilling mutes flavor, so cocktail-format drinks are over-seasoned to compensate), Ice (flavored ice cubes/spheres as a second flavor-delivery vector, borrowed from The Aviary's cocktail book), Clarity (clear = crisp/cleansing; opaque = mouth-coating; milk-punch and consommé-raft clarification techniques are used throughout), Proof/"heat" (alcohol's burn is sometimes simulated with chili, ginger, etc., though the authors admit it's hard to fully replicate).
- **Structural techniques repeated across the book:** sous-vide (or DIY water-bath) infusions at 90°C/195°F for extracting spice/herb/fruit flavor without alcohol; milk-punch clarification (curdling warm milk with acid, straining through the curd bed); consommé/egg-white raft clarification (savory technique repurposed for a sparkling white-chocolate-guava drink); Pectinex/kieselsol/chitosan fining for crystal-clear juices; carbonation via Perlini shaker, home soda carbonators, or CO2/N2O cartridges (including a triple-carbonation "widget" simulation for a stout); flavored ice molds (spheres, cubes) as a secondary flavor-release mechanism.
- **Equipment called out as important:** digital scale (grams throughout — most recipes are weight-based, not volume), Vitamix-grade blender, electric juicer (centrifugal or masticating), Perlini carbonating shaker or a hand soda-carbonator, ice molds, Japanese jiggers.
- **Practical/safety notes:** fresh-ingredient NA drinks can spontaneously ferment (produce real alcohol) if left at room temp — refrigerate, use within ~a week, freeze for longer storage. Standard batch yield is ~1 qt/1 L ("enough to fill a large bottle").

## 3. Recipe Sample — Organized by Section

All recipes below are transcribed with exact quantities/ratios/method as printed.

### A. Basic Syrups (p. 22)
**Simple Syrup:** 100 g sugar + 100 g warm water, whisk to dissolve, refrigerate.
**Demerara Syrup:** 200 g demerara sugar + 100 g water, heat until dissolved, cool, refrigerate.

### B. Back Bar — "Non-Alcoholic Spirits" (yield ≈1 qt each)

**In the Style of GIN** (p. 28)
- Juniper tincture: 300 g vegetable glycerin, 150 g juniper berries, 150 g water — blend briefly, steep overnight refrigerated, strain.
- Botanical base: 750 g water, 17 g coriander seeds, 17 g lemon peel, 5 g fennel seeds, 4 g cinnamon sticks, 3 g fresh ginger, 2 g star anise, 1 g angelica root, 1 g green cardamom, 1 g kosher salt — sous-vide 90°C/1 hr or simmer 1 hr covered; strain, combine 250 g of this with all the juniper tincture.

**In the Style of AMERICAN WHISKEY** (p. 29): 60 g malted barley (oven-toasted 400°F/30 min), 1 banana (roasted whole, unpeeled, until skin blackens), 1500 g water, 75 g dried peaches, 75 g dried figs, 60 g popped popcorn, 45 g charred oak chips, 37 g fenugreek seeds, 34 g molasses, 30 g cinnamon sticks, 2 vanilla beans, 1.5 g salt — combine roasted components + remaining ingredients, sous-vide 90°C/1 hr or simmer 1 hr, strain.

**In the Style of TEQUILA** (p. 30): ½ pineapple (bark only, charred flesh reserved elsewhere), 1000 g water, 40 g apricot, 30 g agave syrup, 20 g roasted peanuts, 12 g black peppercorns, 2 g salt, 2 vanilla beans, 50 g parsley, 25 g ginger, 15 g mint, 9 g lime peel, 7 g jalapeño, 4 g bay leaves — simmer bark+core spices 1 hr, strain, add remaining fresh aromatics, steep 2 hrs room temp, strain again.

**ORANGE LIQUEUR** (inspired by Triple Sec/Cointreau, p. 31): 650 g whole oranges incl. peel (quartered), 550 g water, 450 g sugar, 2 cloves, 4 g cinnamon, 1 vanilla bean — sous-vide 90°C for 4–6 hrs until oranges are very soft, strain.

**In the Style of MEZCAL** (p. 32): charred pineapple bark, 1000 g water, apricot, agave syrup, 25 g liquid smoke, peanuts, coriander, black cardamom, black/white/Szechuan peppercorns, salt, 2 g lapsang souchong tea, parsley, ginger, jalapeño, bay leaves, lime peel — char pineapple bark on grill, simmer with spices 1 hr, strain, steep with fresh aromatics 2 hrs, strain again.

**In the Style of SPANISH RUM** (p. 33): caramelize 60 g sugar, add 3 vanilla beans, 25 g orange peel, 7 g cacao nibs, 3 g nutmeg, 2 g salt, deglaze with 1000 g water, steep 1 hr, strain. *Variant — Blackstrap Rum:* stir in 80 g molasses after cooking.

**ORANGE BITTERS** (inspired by Regans', p. 34): 500 g water, 50 g vegetable glycerin, 25 g gentian root, 20 g coriander, 20 g dried orange peel, 8 g star anise, 5 g cloves, 5 g green cardamom, 4 g cinchona bark, 1.5 g caraway — sous-vide/simmer 1 hr, strain.

**AROMATIC BITTERS** (inspired by Angostura, p. 35): burnt sugar (40 g) base + cloves, cinnamon, gentian root, star anise, dried orange peel, nutmeg, cardamom, ginger powder, allspice, black pepper, vanilla, sassafras/sarsaparilla, 1000 g water — toast spices in burnt sugar, deglaze, simmer 1 hr, strain.

**BITTER AMARO** (inspired by Cynar, p. 36): 1000 g water blended with 500 g whole artichokes, oxidized uncovered 2 hrs (turns black), strained; 700 g of this liquid added incrementally to caramelized 200 g sugar, plus 200 g Coca-Cola, 36 g basil, gentian root, cinchona bark, tarragon, angelica root — simmer 1 hr, strain.

**In the Style of JAMAICAN RUM** (p. 37): roasted bananas + charred pineapple bark, water, lime peel, ginger, toasted oak chips, black cardamom, allspice, cinnamon, salt — sous-vide/simmer 1 hr, strain.

**AMARO** (inspired by Averna, p. 38): caramelized sugar (300 g) + French chicory, cinnamon, coriander, angelica root, fennel seeds, gentian root, anise seeds, cloves, water, orange peel — simmer 1 hr, strain.

**In the Style of SWEET VERMOUTH** (p. 39): caramelized sugar + verjus rouge, vanilla, ginger, dried figs/cherries, orange/lemon peel, raisins, cinnamon, coriander, cardamom, gentian root, cloves, chamomile, cinchona bark, tarragon — simmer 1 hr, strain.

**HERBAL LIQUEUR** (inspired by Chartreuse, p. 40): honey, vegetable glycerin, poblano peppers, ginger, mastic, basil, parsley, galangal, coriander, star anise, fennel pollen, fennel seeds, tarragon, cardamom, thyme, orange/lemon peel, black peppercorns, cinnamon, fenugreek, mint, Szechuan/green peppercorns, cloves, saffron — sous-vide/simmer 1 hr, strain.

**In the Style of FERNET** (p. 41): burnt-sugar base + galangal, cane syrup, fresh & dried mint, aloe vera leaf powder, gentian root, cinchona bark, green cardamom, saffron, water — simmer 1 hr, strain.

**BITTER LIQUEUR** (inspired by Campari, p. 42): 700 g water, 400 g sugar, fresh grapefruit & orange (incl. peel), gentian root, cinnamon, pink peppercorns, star anise, angelica root, cloves, optional red food coloring — sous-vide/simmer 1 hr, strain.

### C. Classic Cocktails (non-alcoholic riffs, using Back Bar components)

**GRAPEFRUIT MIMOSA** (p. 48): 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice, chilled + 4 oz "Lychee Champagne" (p. 194). Build in flute.

**PALOMA** (p. 49): 3 oz NA tequila, 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice, ¾ oz simple syrup, ¾ oz fresh lime juice, grapefruit peel. Shake with ice, strain over ice, express peel.

**DAIQUIRI** (p. 50): 3 oz NA Spanish or Jamaican rum, ¾ oz fresh lime juice, ¾ oz simple syrup, grapefruit peel (expressed, discarded). Shake, strain up.

**JUNGLE BIRD** (p. 51): 2½ oz NA Spanish/Blackstrap rum, 1½ oz fresh pineapple juice, ¾ oz NA bitter liqueur, ½ oz simple syrup, ½ oz fresh lime juice, maraschino cherry + orange peel garnish. Shake, strain over ice.

**MARGARITA** (p. 52): 3 oz NA mezcal, 1 oz NA orange liqueur, ¾ oz fresh lime juice. Salt rim, shake, strain up. *Variant — With Hot Sauce Ice* (p. 53): freeze Fresno-chili juice + water + lime + sugar into ice cubes; serve margarita mix shaken/strained over the spicy ice.

**ABSINTHE / DEATH IN THE AFTERNOON** (p. 55–56): Absinthe syrup = 250 g vegetable glycerin, 24 drops sweet fennel oil, 13 drops lemon oil, 6 drops star anise oil, 1 drop green food coloring (blended). Classic drip: 10 g syrup + 80 g very cold water, added slowly (louches cloudy). *Death in the Afternoon:* ½ oz absinthe syrup + 6 oz "Lychee Champagne."

**FRENCH 75** (p. 57): 3 oz NA gin, ¾ oz fresh lemon juice, ¾ oz simple syrup, shaken and strained into a flute, topped with 3 oz Lychee Champagne, lemon peel garnish.

**WHISKEY SOUR** (p. 58): 3 oz NA American whiskey, ¾ oz fresh lemon juice, ¾ oz simple syrup, 1 egg white — dry shake, shake with ice, strain, express lemon peel.

**BLACK MANHATTAN** (p. 59): 2 oz NA American whiskey, 1 oz NA amaro, 2 dashes NA aromatic bitters. Stir with ice, strain, garnish 2 maraschino cherries.

**AMARO & COKE** (p. 60): 2 oz chilled Coca-Cola + 1 oz NA bitter amaro or fernet, built over ice.

**BIJOU** (p. 61): 1 oz NA gin, 1 oz NA herbal liqueur, 1 oz NA sweet vermouth. Stir, strain.

**NEGRONI** (p. 62): 1 oz NA gin, 1 oz NA sweet vermouth, 1 oz NA bitter liqueur, orange peel. Stir, strain, express peel.

## 4. Notable Cross-Cutting Techniques Worth Highlighting

1. **The "Back Bar" concept as reusable infrastructure** — the 14 non-alcoholic spirit analogs (Gin, American Whiskey, Tequila, Orange Liqueur, Mezcal, Spanish/Blackstrap/Jamaican Rum, Orange Bitters, Aromatic Bitters, Bitter Amaro, Amaro, Sweet Vermouth, Herbal Liqueur, Fernet, Bitter Liqueur) are designed to be interchangeable building blocks, reused across dozens of downstream cocktails — the same design pattern as a real bar's back bar.
2. **Milk-punch clarification** (Banana Chai) and **egg-white raft/consommé clarification** (Sparkling White Chocolate & Guava) are savory/classical techniques deliberately repurposed for sweet, "wine-like" clarity.
3. **Flavored ice as a second act** — fruitcake ice, smoke ice, mint-honeydew ice spheres, hot-sauce ice cubes — are used throughout to let a drink evolve in the glass, replicating a technique from The Aviary's alcoholic cocktail program.
4. **Weight-based (gram) measurement** dominates component recipes; only the final "to assemble and serve" step uses oz/jiggers, since that's the format home bartenders expect.
5. **Carbonation is treated as optional but preferred** — nearly every fizzy drink gives three alternative methods (Perlini shaker / home carbonation device / no equipment, just shake-and-serve flat) so recipes remain accessible without specialty gear.
