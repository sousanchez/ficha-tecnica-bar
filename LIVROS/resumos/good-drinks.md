## 1. Overview and Structure

*Good Drinks* is a journalistic, road-trip-researched cookbook of nonalcoholic mixed drinks. Bainbridge, a food/drinks writer, spent a summer crisscrossing the U.S. interviewing bartenders, restaurateurs, and chefs about their best alcohol-free drinks, then adapted ~50 of those recipes for the home kitchen. Nearly every recipe is attributed to a named bartender and venue (a "guest recipe" anthology format), with Bainbridge's own headnotes, substitution advice, and sourcing tips attached to each.

**Front matter (read in full):**
- **Introduction** — "Of Me" (author's research trip); "Of the Drinks" (terminology debate: Mocktail vs. "Spiritfree" [Julia Momose's term] vs. Bainbridge's own preferred "Good Drinks"); "How This Book Is Structured" (organized by daypart/occasion, not by drink family); "On Making Good Drinks" (two schools: alcohol-free riffs on classics vs. drinks built around a non-alcohol-centric ingredient); "'Alcohol-Free,' Explained" (the legal/historical 0.5% ABV threshold, traced to 1919 Volstead-era Prohibition history); short sections on Fermented Drinks (why kombucha/tepache are excluded — uncontrolled home fermentation can exceed 0.5% ABV) and Bitters (most bitters contain alcohol; Dram and El Guapo make truly alcohol-free versions).
- **Tools** — jigger, Boston shaker, Julep/Hawthorne strainers, Y-peeler, juicers (hand press + centrifugal), blender, carbonator (SodaStream or iSi soda siphon — carbonate BEFORE sweetening to avoid foam/clog/explosion risk), mixing glass and barspoon, spindle drink mixer/swizzle stick.
- **Glassware** — chill glasses for cold drinks when specified.
- **Ingredients** — fresh-squeezed citrus (fine-strained, no pulp); fresh juice generally preferred over store-bought (exceptions: pomegranate/POM, beet juice); soda water vs. club soda (interchangeable for most palates); tonic water (small-batch brands recommended: Fentimans, Fever-Tree, East Imperial, Top Note); dairy and dairy-ish (whole/coconut/almond/condensed milk, kefir, heavy or coconut cream); fresh herbs; verjus (unripe grape juice, red = stone-fruit-like, white = crisp/tart); pantry sweeteners, spices, salt (bartenders use saline solution; home cooks can use a pinch); tea (loose-leaf full-leaf, not "dust and fannings"); vinegars/shrubs; flower waters (orange flower, rose); powdered acids (tartaric, lactic, citric, malic — for acidity without added citrus flavor/volume); nonalcoholic spirits (Seedlip Garden 108, Spice 94, Grove 42 — distilled botanicals, alcohol used only as processing aid then removed).
- **Ice** — three jobs (chill/dilute/ornament); three presentation types (standard cube, "rock"/large cube, crushed); working ice must be fresh (freezer odors transfer).
- **A Few More Notes** — On Mouthfeel (body/weight — eggs, whey, cream add body; sugar adds body; cold liquid feels heavier); On Straws (bamboo/paper/metal); On Inspiration (build drinks around a flavor/texture/mood/journey).

**The Drinks (Part Two), by chapter/daypart:**
1. **But First, Syrup** — 4 base syrup recipes (Simple, Rich Simple, Honey, Rich Honey).
2. **Midday** (10 drinks) — bright, sunny, some caffeinated: Billows & Thieves, Staycation, Jardín Verde, Dollar Slice, Chicha Morada Agua Fresca, The Bicycle Gang, Shiso Spritz, Honeydew-Avocado Agua Fresca, Pimm's Crown, Yu the Great. Plus sidebar recipe "Midnight Magic" and profile "I've Been Drinking: Espresso and Tonic with Lime."
3. **Afternoon** (10 drinks) — refreshing/restorative: U-Me & Everyone We Know, Grapefruit Radler, Cherry Ginger & Coconut Cream Ale, Verjus Spritz, Get Well Soon, The NYC Special, Tomato Water & Mint, Blackberry Cold Brew Colada, Lorca, Nectar of the Gods, Don't Touch My Car Keys. Sidebars: "Nonalcoholic Beer Gets Good," "I've Been Drinking: Limone e Sale."
4. **Happy Hour** (11 drinks) — celebratory/wind-down: King Palm, Buckwheat Champagne, Salted Rosemary Paloma, Shimeji Mushroom Elixir, Pear Cider, Don't Call Me Shirley, Fizzy Hop Tea, Apple & Miso, Like My Father, Padova Spritz, Sunomono. Sidebars: "More on Oils" (essential oils in drinks), "I've Been Drinking: Sanbitter," "More Bitter Sodas to Try."
5. **Evening** (10 drinks) — food-pairing drinks with acid/tannin structure: Squash & Sorghum, Cham-pine, Cucumber Doogh, Change of Address, Up-Beet, Saffron Sharbat, Earth & Leaf, El Curandero, Pea Flower Lemonade, Sparkling Tarragon Cider. Sidebars: "Once More, with Feeling: On Carbonation," "I've Been Drinking: Bitters and Soda Water" + DIY bitters-and-soda method, "I've Been Drinking: Vinegar" + Oregon Berry Drinking Vinegar recipe.
6. **Nighttime** (10 drinks) — dessert/nightcap: Sea Salt Shakerato, Digesteaf, Cold-Fashioned, Golden Hour, Cherry & Smoke, Fuyu, Rose & Kumquat, Rhapsody in Blue, The Rockefeller, The Umstead.

**Back matter:** Bars to Try (directory), Acknowledgments, Index.

## 2. Technique / Philosophy Synthesis

- **Central design problem:** alcohol supplies structure, complexity, bitterness, and body to a cocktail. Remove it and you're left with sugar, acid, and cold water — so every recipe compensates deliberately.
- **Two schools of construction** (per the introduction): (1) build an alcohol-free analogue of a classic cocktail by re-creating its *flavor experience* rather than its literal formula (e.g., The Rockefeller re-creates a Manhattan's weight/warmth/bitterness using spiced vermouth-style syrup + bitter citrus water, not a literal substitution); (2) build a drink around a non-alcoholic centerpiece ingredient (squash, jasmine tea, rosemary) where balance—not booze-replacement—is the goal.
- **Body/mouthfeel fixes:** kefir whey (King Palm), egg or dry-shake foam (Fuyu, Billows & Thieves), cream/coconut cream (Cherry Ginger & Coconut Cream Ale, The Bicycle Gang), yogurt (Cucumber Doogh, The Umstead), tahini (Lorca), sugar concentration, and serving-temperature control (cold liquid reads heavier/more viscous).
- **Acid architecture without booze:** shrubs/drinking vinegars (Shimeji Mushroom Elixir, Nectar of the Gods, Oregon Berry Drinking Vinegar, Tres Chiles Shrub), verjus (unripe grape juice — softer acid than citrus, used as a wine analogue), powdered food-grade acids (tartaric/lactic/malic/citric) to add sharpness without extra volume or secondary citrus flavor (Cham-pine, Vermouth for The Rockefeller, Champagne Acid for Sparkling Tarragon Cider).
- **Bitterness engineering** (replacing alcohol's characteristic bitter edge): tea tannins (black, rooibos, oolong, hōjicha, Lapsang Souchong), hop infusions (Fizzy Hop Tea), amaro-style botanical concentrates (Digesteaf's Astoria's Amaro — chicory root, ashwagandha, artichoke leaf, licorice root, rose petals), DIY tea bitters, gentian root, Italian bitter sodas (Sanbitter, chinotto), and alcohol-free bitters brands (Dram, El Guapo).
- **Umami/savory layering:** miso (Apple & Miso), soy sauce (Tomato Water & Mint, Change of Address), mushroom powder/shrub (Dollar Slice's Spiced Mushroom Salt, Shimeji Mushroom Elixir, The Umstead's Maitake Mushroom Tea), sesame (Sunomono, Fuyu's Black Sesame Cream).
- **Carbonation discipline:** always carbonate before sweetening (sugar creates foam and can clog/rupture a SodaStream); an iSi soda siphon allows pre-sweetened carbonation with the correct technique.
- **Batching:** nearly every recipe includes a "Batch for 4/6/8" scaled version for entertaining, generally built in a pitcher rather than shaken individually.
- **Commitment Level rating system:** each recipe is tagged 1–4 (simple pour, to weekend project with multiple subrecipes and sourced specialty ingredients), letting the cook triage effort against occasion.
- **Sourcing ethos:** many recipes call for specific specialty items (Seedlip, gentian root, soy lecithin, food-grade acids, pandan extract, yuzu kosho, Urfa biber, saba) with named vendors, reflecting a "craft cocktail bar" standard applied to zero-proof drinks rather than treating them as an afterthought.

## 3. Recipe Sample (organized, with exact ingredients/ratios/method)

### Base Syrups (Chapter: But First, Syrup)

**Simple Syrup** (makes 1¾ cups)
1 cup water + 1 cup granulated sugar. Simmer, stirring, 3–5 min until dissolved. Cool. Fridge, 2 weeks.

**Rich Simple Syrup** (makes 1 cup)
½ cup water + 1 cup granulated sugar. Same method. Fridge, 1 month.

**Honey Syrup** (makes 2 cups)
1 cup mild honey + 1 cup water. Same method. Fridge, 2 weeks.

**Rich Honey Syrup** (makes 1½ cups)
1 cup mild honey + ½ cup water. Same method. Fridge, 1 month.

### Midday

**Billows & Thieves** (Mike Di Tota, The Bonnie, Queens NY) — Commitment 1, serves 1
3 oz fresh grapefruit juice, ½ oz fresh lemon juice, 1 oz cold-brew concentrate, ½ oz Black Cardamom–Cinnamon Syrup, pinch smoked sea salt, grated nutmeg garnish.
*Method:* combine in shaker, fill with ice, shake vigorously 20 sec, double-strain into coupe, grate nutmeg on top.
*Black Cardamom–Cinnamon Syrup:* 2 cracked cinnamon sticks + 3 cracked black cardamom pods toasted 2–3 min, then ½ cup water + 1 cup maple syrup simmered 5 min, fine-strained. Keeps 1 week.

**Jardín Verde** (Bryan Dayton, Corrida, Boulder CO) — Commitment 1, serves 1
Cucumber ribbon garnish in wine glass, 2–3 ice cubes, 1½ oz Seedlip Garden 108, 4 oz Fever-Tree Mediterranean Tonic, basil sprig garnish. Batch for 6: 1 cup + 2 Tbsp Seedlip, 3 cups tonic.

**Dollar Slice** (Aaron Polsky, Listen Bar, NY) — Commitment 3, serves 1 — a Bloody Mary riff
Spiced Mushroom Salt rim (1 oz dried shiitake packet blended to powder + 3 Tbsp pizza seasoning + 2 Tbsp hickory-smoked salt), lime wedge; 2 oz Seedlip Spice 94, 4 oz fresh tomato juice, 1 tsp Tabasco Sriracha, pinch kosher salt, ½ oz fresh lime juice; garnish oregano sprig + optional peperoncino.
*Method:* rim glass with salt; combine remaining ingredients in one half of shaker tin, roll (dump back and forth) 3 times over ice to chill/aerate without over-diluting; pour and garnish.

**Yu the Great** (Samantha Azarow, Departure, Portland OR) — Commitment 1, serves 1
1 oz Basil-Matcha Syrup, ¾ oz fresh lime juice, 1 oz full-fat coconut milk (well shaken), 3 oz soda water; matcha powder garnish.
*Method:* shake syrup/lime/coconut milk briefly (~3 sec) with ice, double-strain into ice-filled tumbler, top with soda water (pour slowly — foams), sift matcha on top.
*Basil-Matcha Syrup:* 1½ tsp matcha powder + 1 cup Thai basil leaves (must be Thai, not Italian) + 1 cup sugar + ¾ cup water, blended on high 2 min until smooth/bright green.

### Afternoon

**U-Me & Everyone We Know** (Gabriella Mlynarczyk) — Commitment 2, serves 1
3 oz Tomato-Watermelon Juice (well shaken), ½ oz fresh lemon juice, ¾ tsp umeboshi (ume plum) vinegar, ½ oz Simple Syrup; watermelon garnish.
*Tomato-Watermelon Juice:* juice 4½ cups cubed watermelon + 2 quartered heirloom tomatoes + 3 cups basil leaves through a juicer; fine-strain. Separates on standing — shake before use.

**Grapefruit Radler** (Jeffrey Morgenthaler, Clyde Common) — Commitment 1, serves 1
1½ oz fresh grapefruit juice, ½ oz fresh lemon juice, ½ oz Rich Simple Syrup, 1 oz soda water, 4–5 oz nonalcoholic lager (Busch NA recommended), lemon wedge garnish.
*Method:* combine juices/syrup/soda in pint glass with ice, top with beer, roll between shaker tins to combine gently.

**Tomato Water & Mint** (Matthew Kammerer, Harbor House Inn) — Commitment 2, serves 1
3½ oz Tomato Water, 1¾ oz tonic water, white verjus/rice vinegar to taste, mint sprig garnish.
*Tomato Water:* blend 2½ lb quartered ripe tomatoes + ½ tsp sea salt + ⅓ cup mint + ⅔ cup white verjus + 1 Tbsp rice vinegar + 1 Tbsp white soy sauce to a salsa consistency; barely simmer 5–8 sec; drip-strain through cheesecloth (double-pass technique) ~45 min to clarify. Use within 1 day.

**Don't Touch My Car Keys** (Shelby Allison, Lost Lake, Chicago) — Commitment 2, serves 1 — tiki-style, needs spindle mixer/swizzle stick
1½ oz Coconut Syrup, ¾ oz fresh lime juice, 3 oz soda water, 5+ dashes aromatic bitters (Fee Brothers Old Fashioned or Angostura), mint garnish.
*Method:* build over crushed ice in Pearl Diver/collins glass, aerate with spindle mixer 5 sec (or swizzle ~20 sec until glass frosts), float bitters on top ("full halo"), garnish.
*Coconut Syrup:* 1 (13½ oz) can unsweetened coconut milk + 2 cups turbinado sugar, warmed until dissolved.

### Happy Hour

**King Palm** (Tyson Buhler, Death & Co, Denver) — Commitment 4, serves 1
1½ oz kefir whey (strained overnight from a quart of plain kefir through 8 layers of cheesecloth), 1½ oz clear coconut water, 1 tsp Pineapple Syrup, 1 tsp Cinnamon Syrup, grapefruit twist (expressed, discarded).
*Method:* stir all with ice 15 sec, strain into chilled Nick & Nora.
*Pineapple Syrup:* ½ cup fresh pineapple juice + ½ cup sugar, blended 2 min. *Cinnamon Syrup:* 4 cinnamon sticks + 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water, boiled and steeped 15 min, strained.

**Buckwheat Champagne** (Michael Kudra, Quince, SF) — Commitment 4, serves 6 — modernist technique
3 Tbsp buckwheat (soba-cha) tea steeped in 3 cups just-boiled water 2–3 min, fine-strained; + pinch flaky salt + 6 drops Lemon Oleo Saccharum; chill; carbonate; stir in 1½ oz Simple Syrup; top each of 6 flutes with 1 tsp Lemon Air.
*Lemon Oleo Saccharum:* zest of 8 lemons (wide strips) macerated with ½ cup sugar 3 hr–1 day, pressed and fine-strained.
*Lemon Air:* 5 oz fresh lemon juice + scant ½ tsp soy lecithin powder + 1 Tbsp 2 tsp water, blended with immersion blender at 45° angle 20–30 sec until foamed; rest 1 min before using.

**Salted Rosemary Paloma** (Naren Young, Dante NY) — Commitment 2, serves 1
½ oz Salted Rosemary Syrup, 2–3 oz fresh grapefruit juice, ½ oz fresh lime juice, 3 oz soda water, grapefruit slice garnish.
*Salted Rosemary Syrup:* 3 Tbsp sugar + 1 tsp Maldon salt + 1 rosemary sprig (cut in 3) + 3 oz water, warmed to dissolve, fine-strained.

**Apple & Miso** (Sean Umstead, Kingfisher, Durham NC) — Commitment 2, serves 1
½ cup apple juice, 2 Tbsp Miso Syrup, ⅛ tsp citric acid, shiso leaf garnish.
*Miso Syrup:* seeds of 1 vanilla bean + ¼ cup shiso leaves + ½ cup sugar + ½ cup just-boiled water blended 1 min; add ¼ cup red miso, blend 30 sec more; strain through cheesecloth.

**Sunomono** (Sean Umstead, Kingfisher) — Commitment 2, serves 1
Cucumber ribbon liner, 3 oz fresh cucumber juice, 1 oz Sesame and Salted Rice Vinegar Shrub, 4–5 oz soda water.
*Shrub:* toast 1 tsp sesame seeds; simmer with 1¼ cups rice vinegar + ¼ tsp kosher salt + ¾ cup sugar 1 min; blend on high 30 sec; rest 30 min; fine-strain.

### Evening

**Squash & Sorghum** (Brad Langdon, The Dabney, DC) — Commitment 3, serves 1
1½ oz Squash and Sorghum Shrub + 4 oz soda water over one large ice cube.
*Shrub:* juice 1 butternut squash, fine-strain, rest overnight to settle starch, decant off starch; simmer juice with 8 crushed black peppercorns until reduced by half (10–15 min); stir in pinch salt + 2 oz apple cider vinegar + 2½ oz sorghum molasses + 1½ oz water; strain through cheesecloth.

**Cham-pine** (Atera, NYC) — Commitment 4, serves 8 — foraged-pine modernist drink
4 cups packed fresh white pine needles simmered (barely, not boiling) in 6¼ cups water 2½ hr; double-strain. Reserve ¼ cup hot pine tea, dissolve ¼ cup sugar + ¼ cup honey into it (syrup, set aside). Into remaining warm tea, stir ¾ tsp tartaric acid + ¾ tsp lactic acid + ¼ tsp malic acid; chill. Carbonate the pine infusion, then gently stir in the syrup; serve in 8 flutes.

**Pea Flower Lemonade** (Nick Wiseman, Little Sesame, DC) — Commitment 1, serves 6 — color-changing drink
Toast 2¼ tsp coriander seeds, crush. Steep with 2½ Tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers + ½ cup turbinado sugar in 6 cups just-boiled water 30 min (liquid is blue); fine-strain, chill. To serve: divide among 6 ice-filled collins glasses, add 1½ tsp fresh lemon juice to each glass at the table — the acid turns the blue liquid purple.

**Sparkling Tarragon Cider** (Piper Kristensen, Oxalis, Brooklyn) — Commitment 2, serves 8
25 tarragon sprigs steeped in 1 cup just-boiled water 30 min, strained; combine with 3 cups apple juice (Martinelli's) + 3 oz Champagne Acid; chill; carbonate; serve in white wine glasses.
*Champagne Acid:* ¼ tsp lactic acid + ¼ tsp tartaric acid whisked into 3 oz water.

### Nighttime

**Cold-Fashioned** (Jonathan Echeverry, Paper Plane Coffee Co.) — Commitment 1, serves 1
½ oz turbinado simple syrup, 1 half-moon orange slice, 1 maraschino cherry, 3 dashes Angostura bitters — muddled in shaker; add 4 oz cold-brew concentrate, shake briefly (~3 sec) to combine; strain into rocks glass over one large ice cube; orange twist garnish.

**Golden Hour** (Jon Palmer, Bachelor Farmer, Minneapolis) — Commitment 2, serves 1 — Sauternes-style dessert drink
2 oz white verjus, ¾ oz Rich Honey Syrup, ¼ oz Simple Syrup, ½ tsp orange flower water. Stir with ice 15 sec, strain into chilled coupe.

**Fuyu** (Olivia Noren & Jonathan Macahon, Yūgen, Chicago) — Commitment 3, serves 1 — eggnog-style
2 oz Seedlip Spice 94, 1 oz Black Sesame Cream, 1½ oz Hōjicha-Ginger Syrup, 1 egg (optional); black sesame garnish.
*Method:* dry-shake all (with egg) 10–15 sec, then add ice and shake again 10–15 sec; double-strain into coupe.
*Black Sesame Cream:* ¼ cup black sesame seeds simmered in 1 cup heavy cream, strained. *Hōjicha-Ginger Syrup:* 1 cup dark muscovado sugar + ⅓ cup loose hōjicha tea simmered in 1 cup water, steeped 30 min, then ½ cup fresh ginger juice stirred in and fine-strained.

**The Rockefeller** (Jermaine Whitehead, Deep Dive, Seattle) — Commitment 4, serves 1 — Manhattan riff, stirred
¾ oz house Vermouth, 3 oz Citrus Water. Stir with ice 15 sec, strain into chilled Nick & Nora, garnish Luxardo cherry.
*Vermouth:* 2¼ tsp juniper berries, ¾ tsp cloves, ¾ tsp fennel seeds, ¾ tsp black peppercorns, 1½ tsp crushed Urfa biber, ¼ tsp vanilla extract, ¼ tsp citric acid, 2 cinnamon sticks simmered 30 min with 1½ cups red verjus + ¾ cup sugar; fine-strained.
*Citrus Water:* peels of 8 oranges, 8 lemons, 4 grapefruits (no pith) simmered with 1 whole nutmeg, 1 tsp cloves, 1 cinnamon stick, 1 Tbsp vanilla extract in 4 cups water at a bare simmer until reduced to 3 cups (~45 min); fine-strained.
