# Little Book of Training — Summary (pages 1-5)

The opening pages of Indie Bartender's "Little Book of Training" lay out the role and standards expected of an In Store Trainer (IST). Page 1 is the table of contents, outlining topics from the IST role and certification through learning styles, training methods, bar skills, and guest interaction. Pages 2-4 define the IST role in detail: how bartenders apply and get certified for the position (manager review, development plan, application to a BDM, interview, and certification), the role's core mission of developing bartenders who consistently deliver great drinks, service, and atmosphere, and a long list of key responsibilities (setting training and test schedules, marking tests and spot checks with 100% accuracy, coordinating training with the GM, and conducting quarterly training needs analysis). It then details the person specification an IST must meet across knowledge, productivity, composure, communication, teamwork, guest service, prioritization, and attitude — emphasizing product/recipe mastery, health and safety awareness, reliability, calm professionalism, and a positive, solution-oriented mindset. Page 4 introduces IST Certification, based on the "Four Key Points of Bartending" (serving consistently high-quality drinks through knowledge, skills, and interaction), requiring a final pour test, a Top 130 & Mixers test (98% minimum pass mark), a delivered one-on-one training session, and front bar certification, alongside the company's core values (People, Energy, Passion, Pride, Excellence, Rock 'n' Roll). Page 5 begins the "Five Key Points of Training" section, covering Planning (90% planning vs. 10% delivery), Communication (two-way feedback and record-keeping), and Flexibility (adapting training style to each trainee's learning needs).
